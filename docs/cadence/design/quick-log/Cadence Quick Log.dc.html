Rules & calls
Which shelf opens
Planned if the week names a dish for this slot, else Usual. The Planned tab is absent, not greyed, when there's nothing — three tabs that day.
What “Usual” ranks on
Foods and one-portion recipes logged at this slot, most-often first, recency as tiebreak (useUsualAtSlot already does this). No kind markers — the shelf you're on already says what these are.
Leftovers
Any logged recipe with yield > 1 in the last 4 days, showing portions still unaccounted for (“2 of 4 left”). Tapping logs one portion and decrements. Gone when it hits zero or ages out.
Tap grammar
Tap = +1 default serving (badge). Tap again = ×2. Hold = amount sheet (1b). × on the chip removes. No row ever opens a form on first tap.
Advice lives elsewhere
One door: Cadence's mark in the title row opens a chat scoped to this meal — the cart (or what was logged) is already in her context, so the first question needs no setup. Nothing else on this drawer talks. The coach's read of the meal arrives in chat after the log, never in the logging path. The hatched preview on the macros is the only “advice” — and it's just arithmetic.
