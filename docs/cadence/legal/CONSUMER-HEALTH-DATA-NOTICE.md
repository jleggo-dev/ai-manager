## 1. What “consumer health data” can mean here

Depending on state definitions, Cadence may process data that regulators treat as consumer health
or similarly sensitive wellness information, including:

- Body-related baseline (for example age, height, weight)
- Constraints you share about what we work around (physical or mind-adjacent)
- Nutrition logs, dietary profile, macro targets, recipes, and meal plans
- Meal / label / fridge photos
- Activity and session logs tied to movement or recovery
- Coach messages that discuss health, food, mood, stress, grief, or similar topics
- Optional location used with weather when it relates to outdoor activity planning

We collect this primarily because **you provide it** while using the coach, or it is generated
from features you choose (logging, photos, chat).

## 2. How we use and share it

**Use:** to provide coaching — remember context, build and bend plans, log food and sessions,
personalize situational context (for example weather), and operate safety boundaries (including
crisis deferral to human help such as 988 in the US).

**Share:** with processors needed to run Cadence (auth/database/storage, hosting, AI/LLM and
vision providers, weather, food reference sources), as described in the Privacy Policy. We do
**not** sell this data for money and do not use it for third-party advertising networks in the
current product.

**Confirm-before-commit:** material plan commitments are meant to follow your confirmation —
aligned with Cadence’s brand, not silent clinical charting.

## 3. Your rights (state CHD-style)

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Know whether we collect consumer health data about you
- Access that data
- Request deletion
- Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
- Appeal a refusal (where the statute provides an appeal)

**How to exercise:** email **`[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`** with “Consumer Health Data Request” in the
subject line and enough information for us to verify you. We will respond within the timeframes
required by applicable law.

**In-product tools:**

- **Start over** erases much coaching data but is **not** full account deletion; some fields may
  remain (see Privacy Policy). For CHD deletion that goes further, contact `[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`.
- **Export:** no self-serve export API yet — request access/export via `[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`;
  fulfillment may be limited until a dedicated tool ships.

## 4. Geofencing / sensitive locations

We do not intentionally collect precise consumer health data about your visits to sensitive
locations (for example medical facilities) for advertising. Optional **home location** is coarse
and used for coaching context such as weather — not for selling location-based ads.
